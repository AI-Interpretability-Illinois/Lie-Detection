Perplexity is one cost; general capability is another. At the options of Table~\ref{tab:main}, plain MMLU accuracy changes by $-$3.4 points under the edit on average, against $-$1.8 under steering and $-$1.3 under the honest prompt. Re-selecting every method with an MMLU check, accuracy within 5 points of the unedited model, leaves the edit's average lead at +9.9 and +5.4 points, and a 2-point check at +7.7 and +6.5, all with intervals above zero (Appendix~\ref{app:review}).
