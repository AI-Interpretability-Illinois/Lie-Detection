Rebuilt with other selection rules at the same families (Appendix~\ref{app:review}), the edit's effect comes from its few positive-score weights: they alone reach 85.0\% on \LB{} against 85.0\% for the full mask, the near-zero padding alone 65.1\%; per-token norms, which remove prompt length, give 83.1\% and a scale-free ratio 85.5\%, and a standardized difference without threshold 66.3\%.
